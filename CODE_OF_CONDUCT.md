# Code of Conduct

Contributors must keep technical and legal review evidence-based, specific and
professional. Critique claims and changes, not people.

The following are unacceptable:

- publishing personal, client, privileged or confidential information;
- fabricating authority, tests, adoption, credentials or review approval;
- harassment, threats or discriminatory abuse;
- knowingly introducing unsafe automation or hiding a material limitation;
- pressuring maintainers to bypass required legal or engineering review.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove material that violates these rules and may
restrict participation. Report sensitive conduct concerns through a private
channel rather than a public issue.
