# Provenance

Huang Minghuan is the founding maintainer and proposed author of record for the
two legal workflow Skills. The original contribution is the workflow design:
task modes, field locks, staged release gates, exact-diff controls, evidence and
page-number separation, stable source IDs, and layered damages, insurance and
payment reconciliation. The public repository also contains original
repository-specific validation, synthetic tests, examples, governance and
packaging material.

OpenAI tools assisted with drafting, restructuring, code generation, testing
and packaging. That assistance does not transfer ownership of official legal
materials or third-party projects, and it does not replace the author's
responsibility to verify authorship, accuracy, permissions and the final public
submission. A submission-specific, unsigned confirmation draft is maintained
in `submission/author-rights-confirmation.md`.

The directory logo was generated specifically for this project with OpenAI
image-generation assistance and no reference image supplied to the generation
call; the composer icon is a resize of the same artwork. The generation and
review boundary is recorded in `submission/brand-asset-provenance.md`.

The public release deliberately excludes:

- the third-party `legal-logic-analysis` Skill because its canonical upstream
  and rights chain were not sufficiently documented for this release;
- the `yaosushi-suzhuang` template bundle because the redistribution rights and
  embedded document metadata of the 67 forms require separate resolution;
- historical matter notes, client-specific preferences, local paths, private
  build dependencies, and real case fixtures;
- official court DOCX/PDF forms. The repository links to official sources but
  does not redistribute those forms.

Official laws, judicial interpretations, notices, forms, and external websites
remain subject to their own legal status and terms. The project's Apache-2.0
license applies only to material the project is authorized to license. See
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and each Skill's source registry.
