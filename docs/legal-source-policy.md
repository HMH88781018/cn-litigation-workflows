# Legal-source policy

Repository material is a workflow snapshot, not a permanent statement of
current law.

For every time-sensitive legal or filing proposition:

1. prefer the legislature, Supreme People's Court, receiving court, regulator,
   or other competent official issuer;
2. record title, issuer, official HTTPS URL, promulgation/effective date,
   verification date, and any transition rule;
3. distinguish binding authority, official form guidance, local technical
   practice, contractual text, and an internal audit heuristic;
4. keep dynamic statistics, insurance limits, interest rates, and local values
   as sourced parameters rather than fixed assumptions;
5. treat a stale or unreachable source as a review trigger, never as permission
   to invent or silently replace a rule.

Source registries live beside the relevant Skill. Their refresh threshold is an
engineering reminder, not a claim that a source remains legally current until
the threshold expires.

Automation may detect age, link failure, or textual change. It must never
rewrite legal instructions or merge a legal-source update automatically.
Changes require exact source evidence, transition analysis, deterministic tests,
engineering review, and qualified legal-content review.
