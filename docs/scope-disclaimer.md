# Scope and limitations

The project supports selected mainland-China litigation document workflows. It
does not provide legal representation, establish an attorney-client
relationship, determine that a filing will be accepted, or replace a lawyer's
case-specific judgment.

It cannot independently verify:

- authenticity or completeness of submitted materials;
- current law, local filing practice, or the applicable court template;
- factual conflicts, litigation strategy, causation, appraisal conclusions, or
  evidentiary weight;
- policy wording, coverage, liability allocation, or recoverable damages;
- signatures, authority, service, deadlines, or filing-channel requirements.

Passing repository tests proves only that defined structural checks succeeded.
It is not substantive legal approval. Real outputs remain `DRAFT` until the
applicable gates, official-source checks, calculations, cross-file reconciliation,
rendering, and human review all pass.
