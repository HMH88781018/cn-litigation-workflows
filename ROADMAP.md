# Roadmap

## 0.1 — public foundation

- two focused Skills with explicit routing;
- deterministic structural and privacy checks;
- synthetic tests and release automation;
- public governance and source policy.

## 0.2 — evidence and formula hardening

- public, reproducible workbook generator;
- more insurance, payment-offset and multiple-dependent boundary cases;
- evidence-booklet manifest and page-range fixture generation;
- documented compatibility matrix for Word, LibreOffice and mobile preview.

## 0.3 — source maintenance

- monthly advisory link/freshness report;
- versioned official-source registry;
- public review record for rule changes;
- no automated legal-rule replacement or merge.

## 1.0 — stable workflow contract

- sustained real-world pilot evidence;
- stable cross-Skill data contract;
- documented migration policy;
- external contributor and adopter review.

Roadmap items are intentions, not promises or evidence of completed work.
